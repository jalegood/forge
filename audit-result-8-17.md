## Weakest workflow

**Init is the weakest workflow, and its weakness is structural rather than incidental.** forge-init.md is a ~1,900-line file that is mostly payload, and the Embedded Payload Synchronization rule that governs it is enforced for exactly four of its blocks — everything outside `test-init-scripts.sh`'s marker list (check-ux-spec.js, all seven templates, the CLAUDE.md block, the completion-report list) has demonstrably drifted, in one case regressing a shipped bug-fix (TASK-050's fence-awareness) back into every future scaffolded project. The pipeline's own history shows why: three separate tasks (TASK-043, TASK-054, TASK-062) each patched _some_ embeds and missed others, and the misses were only ever caught by later observations, never by a mechanism. Plan is the runner-up and arguably the higher-leverage fix: its two high-severity findings (the unmatched VISION stub literal and the always-passing awk gate) are both _generators_ of the vacuous-gate failure mode the project has now logged five instances of — but plan's defects are two lines each, while init's defect is an enforcement model that covers a shrinking fraction of a growing file. TASK-069/070 will narrow init's gap; nothing currently planned closes the check-ux-spec.js embed, the CLAUDE.md block, or the VERSION provisioning step, so those three are the audit's most actionable output.
